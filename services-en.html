What You Can Ask Us

Five areas of work

Each one is about taking Japanese culture and putting it in a form that travels. Pricing is quoted per project. It is fine to come to us before anything is decided.

01 — Corporate Masu

Engraved masu, gifts and corporate novelties

Anniversary gifts, corporate novelties, wedding favours, opening celebrations, and cups for restaurant service. We engrave your name or logo into Japanese hinoki cypress masu and build the spec around how it will be used. From one piece, ten for companies. Before you order, we send a quote and a preview of the finished engraving, free.

  • Anniversary gifts
  • Logo novelties
  • Wedding favours
  • Restaurant use
  • Gifts for abroad
MASU-STORE for companies ↗

02 — Architectural

Masu walls and wooden interiors (FOMUS ARCHITECTURAL)

One-go masu supplied as modules for walls, partitions, columns and fixtures — for hotel lobbies, restaurants, office entrances and retail interiors. Available piece by piece or as assembled panels. Glass coating and flame-retardant treatment can be arranged.

  • Retail interiors
  • Japandi wall
  • Hotel lobbies
  • Partitions
  • Display fixtures

03 — Overseas

Overseas promotion support

We carry Japanese sake, crafts and regional products to embassy receptions, Japanese cultural events and local communities abroad, put them in people's hands, and bring back photos, video and honest reactions — down to labels and copy rewritten for the local language.

  • Sake abroad
  • Craft distribution
  • Regional products
  • On-site reports
  • Multilingual labels
See overseas support ›

04 — Photography

Photography (events, products, overseas)

Event records, product and brand photography, and shoots abroad. Twenty countries of practice at asking strangers, in languages we do not speak, if we may take their picture. Files come back finished and ready for your site and social channels.

  • Event photography
  • Product photography
  • Brand imagery
  • Overseas shoots
  • On-location
See photography ›

05 — Web & AI

Multilingual websites, AI and automation

Multilingual websites, AI chatbots for shops and companies, and automation for work now done by hand. We build and run every one of our own services in house, so what you get is working software, not a deck.

  • Multilingual sites
  • AI chatbots
  • Automation
  • E-commerce
  • Regional DX
See web & AI ›

Projects often cross several of these. Make a product for an overseas market, test it on the ground, then build the multilingual site around it — that whole line can run through one team.

FAQ

Questions

QHow much does it cost?

It depends on the work, so we quote per project, and quoting is free. Engraved masu have a published floor: orders start at 4,400 yen including tax and design.

QWill you take small jobs?

Yes. One masu, a half-day shoot, or a single web page.

QWhere do you work?

Masu production, web and AI work happen anywhere. For photography we are based in Japan and travel, including abroad, depending on the job.

QWe have not decided what to make yet.

That is the most common way people arrive. "We want to go abroad but do not know where to start" is a fine place to begin.

QCan we pay by invoice?

Yes, invoicing is available for companies.
