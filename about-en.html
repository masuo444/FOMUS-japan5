About FOMUS

From Masu, to the future of Japanese culture.

FOMUS brings Japanese culture to the world through four businesses — craft, technology, entertainment and community — with the traditional Japanese masu as the doorway.

MaSU — Founder & Creative Director
Keisuke Masuo / MaSU

Founder of FOMUS. Active across more than 40 countries, he promotes the value of Japanese culture globally through exhibitions at Japanese embassies abroad, cultural outreach, and the creation of international communities.

Working across craft, AI, and creative disciplines, he leads AI adoption support for businesses, web development, and cultural projects — drawing on his background in photography, film, and design to shape the future of Japanese culture.
