The Masu

An object whose name means “to prosper.”

The masu (枡) is a square box of hinoki cypress. For more than 1,300 years it was used in Japan to measure rice and sake. Its name sounds like masu (増す) — “to increase, to prosper” — so it became an auspicious object, used at celebrations and given as a gift of good fortune.

Masu Wall turns hundreds of these boxes into one surface: warm hinoki, a precise grid and a quiet play of light and shadow. It brings the restraint of Japanese interior design — the calm of wabi, the honesty of natural wood — into hotels, restaurants and homes, with a meaning of welcome and prosperity built in.

How It Works

You design the wall. We supply the masu.

Masu Wall is sold as modules. Architects, designers and owners work with the masu like any other material, and FOMUS delivers them from Japan.

  1. STEP 01

    Design your wall

    You or your architect / interior designer design the pattern, layout and backing to suit the space. If you would like, FOMUS can prepare a design proposal (optional, charged separately).

  2. STEP 02

    Choose quantity & specs

    Decide the number of masu and the specification: individual modules or panel type, glass coating, fire-retardant treatment, engraving.

  3. STEP 03

    Made in Japan

    The masu modules are produced in Japan in Japanese hinoki, individually or pre-assembled as panels.

  4. STEP 04

    Shipped by FOMUS

    FOMUS arranges shipping from Japan to your country.

  5. STEP 05

    Installed by your contractor

    Your local contractor installs the wall. FOMUS provides installation instructions, and can also work with your local contractor.

Need help with the design? A pattern and layout proposal by FOMUS is available as an option, charged separately. The standard service is the supply of masu modules for your own design.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

QDo you ship worldwide, including Saudi Arabia and the UAE?

Yes. FOMUS arranges shipping from Japan to your country, including Saudi Arabia, the UAE and other Gulf countries. Import procedures differ by destination, so please consult us on import and delivery terms for your project.

QWho designs the wall?

You do — or your architect or interior designer. Masu Wall is supplied as modules for your own design. If you would like help, FOMUS can prepare a design proposal as an option, charged separately.

QWho installs the wall?

Your local contractor installs the wall. FOMUS provides installation instructions and can also work with your local contractor. Masu can be supplied individually or as pre-assembled panels.

QIs it fire-safe?

Fire-retardant treatment of the wood is available on request. Requirements differ by country and building, so please consult us on your local requirements early in the project.

QCan we add our logo or Arabic calligraphy?

Yes. Laser engraving of logos, names and Arabic calligraphy or text is available on request, as are gold leaf and other branding.

QWhat is the minimum order and price? How long does it take?

Price, minimum quantity and lead time are on request. They depend mainly on the number of masu and the finishing, so please tell us about your project and your target opening or handover date.
